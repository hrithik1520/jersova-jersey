Who We Are

Every Stitch Has A Reason.

Jersova Jersey is a custom sportswear studio specializing in fully sublimated jerseys, shorts and training pants for football, futsal and academy teams.

We handle everything in-house — design, print, cut and sew — so every order gets the same obsessive attention whether it's a 5-a-side squad or a 40-player academy roster. No minimum-order gatekeeping, no generic templates recolored and reshipped. Just kit that fits the identity you're building.

FAQ

Common Questions.

What's the minimum order quantity?+

There's no strict minimum — we've produced single jerseys and full 40-player academy orders. Bulk pricing kicks in from 10+ units.

Can I use my own design instead of a template?+

Absolutely. Send your artwork, colors or reference images and our designers will build a fully custom mockup around it.

How long does production take?+

Most orders ship within 2–3 weeks of design approval, depending on order size and season demand.

Do you ship internationally?+

Yes — we currently ship to 24 countries with tracked delivery included on every order.

Are names, numbers and crests included?+

Yes, player names, squad numbers and club crests are included at no extra cost on every jersey.
